# 01 — Product overview

## 1.1 Vision
FripCash is a **Guinea-focused marketplace** for buying and selling goods (fashion, home, electronics, grocery / proximity commerce, partner enseignes). Payments are oriented around **Orange Money** and amounts are in **GNF**.

## 1.2 Problem solved
- Individuals sell second-hand items safely with escrow-style release.  
- Shops sell new goods with clear commission and delivery rules.  
- Neighborhood (“proximité”) shops and large partenaires (“enseignes”) need **manual validation** before full public visibility.  
- Couriers fulfill city deliveries for non-proximity orders.

## 1.3 Product principles
1. **One person = one account**; capabilities are **added** (upgrade), not freely swapped.  
2. **Buyer history never disappears** when someone becomes a seller.  
3. **Listing destination** (home universe) is determined by seller profile, not random per-item choice.  
4. **Server is authoritative** for money, roles, verification, and dispute outcomes.  
5. **App = full seller/courier depth**; **web = browse + light account / light particulier sell**; **admin = ops**.

## 1.4 Success criteria (product)
- User can register, verify phone, browse universes, buy with Orange Money flow, track order.  
- Seller can upgrade, publish (when allowed), manage sales, withdraw.  
- Admin can validate shops, moderate listings, resolve disputes, configure zones/fees/commissions.  
- Courier can accept missions and complete delivery steps.

## 1.5 Currency & locale
- Currency: **GNF** (no decimals in UX; integers).  
- Phone: Guinea **+224**.  
- Delivery context: Conakry zones / quartiers (configurable via admin).
